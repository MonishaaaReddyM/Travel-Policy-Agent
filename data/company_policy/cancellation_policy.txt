UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE CANCELLATION POLICY
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Mobility Operations

1. Purpose
This sample policy explains how cancelled Uber business trips should be handled.

2. Employee Cancellation
Employees should cancel a ride as soon as they know it is no longer required.

3. Cancellation Charges
A cancellation fee may be reimbursable when:
- The ride was booked for an approved business purpose.
- The cancellation was necessary because the business requirement changed.
- The charge is supported by the trip record.

4. Personal Cancellations
Cancellation fees resulting from personal or non-business bookings are not reimbursable.

5. Driver Cancellation
When a driver cancels an eligible business trip, the employee may request another ride. Any resulting cancellation charge should be reviewed against the trip record.

6. Repeated Cancellations
Repeated cancellations may be flagged for review by the travel administrator.

7. Required Information
A cancellation review should include:
- Employee ID
- Trip ID, if available
- Cancellation date
- Cancellation reason
- Cancellation fee
- Business purpose

8. Important Note
This document does not define how Uber calculates the actual cancellation fee. The assistant should not invent a fee amount when it is not present in the available data.
