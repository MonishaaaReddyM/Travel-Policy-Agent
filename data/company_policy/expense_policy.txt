UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE EXPENSE POLICY
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Finance Operations

1. Purpose
This sample policy defines how employees should handle Uber business travel expenses.

2. Reimbursable Amount
The reimbursable amount is the lower of:
- The actual trip amount.
- The applicable country or trip spending limit.

3. Country Limits
India:
Standard individual ride limit = INR 2,000.

United States:
Standard individual ride limit = USD 75.

4. Trips Above the Limit
If the actual trip amount exceeds the applicable limit, the employee must obtain the required approval before the excess amount can be considered reimbursable.

5. Expense Submission
An expense submission should contain:
- Employee ID
- Trip date
- Trip amount
- Currency
- Business purpose
- Trip type
- Approval reference, if applicable

6. Missing Information
Expenses with missing employee ID, trip amount, or business purpose should be flagged for review.

7. Example
If an eligible India business trip costs INR 1,500, the reimbursable amount is INR 1,500.
If the trip costs INR 2,500 and no additional approval exists, only INR 2,000 is within the standard policy limit and the remaining INR 500 requires review.
