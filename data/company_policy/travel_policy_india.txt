UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE TRAVEL POLICY
Country: India
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Corporate Travel Team

1. Purpose
This sample policy defines when employees may use company-sponsored Uber rides for business travel in India.

2. Eligible Business Trips
Employees may use company-sponsored rides for:
- Travel between home and an airport when the trip is related to approved business travel.
- Travel between company offices and approved business locations.
- Travel to customer or partner meetings.
- Other business travel approved by the employee's manager.

3. Restricted Trips
Company-sponsored rides should not be used for:
- Personal leisure trips.
- Trips unrelated to company business.
- Travel for family members unless explicitly approved.
- Commutes on days when the employee is not working, unless the trip is part of approved business travel.

4. Standard Trip Limit
The standard reimbursement limit for an individual business ride is INR 2,000 per trip.
Trips above this limit require additional approval according to the Approval Policy.

5. Late-Night Travel
Business travel between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM is permitted when it is related to an approved business activity. Additional approval may be required when the trip exceeds the applicable spending limit.

6. Required Information
Employees should provide a valid employee ID, business purpose, trip date, and cost when submitting an expense.

7. Policy Interpretation
When two policy rules appear to conflict, the stricter spending or approval requirement should be followed until clarification is obtained from the travel administrator.
