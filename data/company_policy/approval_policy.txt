UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE TRAVEL APPROVAL POLICY
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Corporate Travel Team

1. Purpose
This sample policy defines when additional approval is required for company-sponsored Uber travel.

2. When Approval Is Required
Additional approval is required when:
- A trip exceeds the standard country spending limit.
- An employee has an "Approval Required" eligibility status.
- A policy explicitly states that manager approval is required.

3. Standard Spending Limits
India: INR 2,000 per individual ride.
United States: USD 75 per individual ride.

4. Approval Hierarchy
First level: Employee's manager.
Second level: Travel administrator, when required by the business policy.

5. Approval Evidence
The approval record should contain:
- Employee ID
- Approver name or ID
- Approval date
- Trip or expense reference
- Approved amount or exception

6. Pending Approval
If approval is required but no approval record exists, the trip should be classified as "Needs Approval" rather than "Approved."

7. Example
EMP002 is marked "Approval Required." Even if the trip amount is below the normal country limit, the employee must obtain the required approval before using the company-sponsored travel program.

8. Assistant Behaviour
The assistant should never claim that an approval exists unless an approval record is available.
