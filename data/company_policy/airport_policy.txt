UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE AIRPORT TRAVEL POLICY
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Airport Travel Program

1. Purpose
This sample policy defines rules for company-sponsored Uber trips to and from airports.

2. Eligible Airport Trips
Airport trips are eligible when:
- The employee is travelling for an approved business purpose.
- The airport trip is connected to an approved business journey.
- The employee uses the company-sponsored business profile.

3. Airport Trip Limits
India:
- Standard limit: INR 2,000 per trip.
- Trips above INR 2,000 require additional approval.

United States:
- Standard limit: USD 75 per trip.
- Trips above USD 75 require additional approval.

4. Late-Night Airport Trips
Airport trips between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM are allowed when connected to approved business travel. The normal spending and approval limits still apply.

5. Personal Airport Trips
Airport rides for personal holidays or other non-business travel are not reimbursable unless an explicit exception has been approved.

6. Documentation
The expense record should include:
- Employee ID
- Airport or travel location
- Trip date
- Business purpose
- Trip amount
- Approval reference, when additional approval is required

7. Example
An employee in India takes an approved business airport trip costing INR 1,800. The trip is within the standard limit and does not require additional spending approval.
