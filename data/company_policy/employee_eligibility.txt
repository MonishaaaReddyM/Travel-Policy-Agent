UBER FOR BUSINESS - SAMPLE EMPLOYEE ELIGIBILITY POLICY
Policy Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Policy Owner: Corporate Travel Team

1. Purpose
This sample policy defines which employees can use company-sponsored Uber business travel.

2. Eligibility Status
Employees can have one of three statuses:
- Eligible: The employee can use the company-sponsored travel program subject to trip policies.
- Not Eligible: The employee cannot use the company-sponsored travel program.
- Approval Required: The employee may use the program only after the required approval is obtained.
