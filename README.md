# AI Travel & Policy Assistant

**Status:** Day 1 of 5 — Project Setup, Git & Policy Knowledge Base

## Business Problem
Employees need a fast, reliable way to understand company travel policies
(country spending limits, airport trip rules, approval requirements,
expense reimbursement, cancellations) and to validate whether a specific
trip is compliant — without manually reading multiple policy documents.

## Solution (in progress)
An AI-powered assistant that combines:
- **RAG** over the company's fictional travel policy documents, so answers
  are grounded in the actual policy text with source attribution.
- **Tools** for employee eligibility checks, trip validation, and
  reimbursement calculation.
- **An agent** that decides whether a question needs policy retrieval,
  a tool call, or both.
- **Conversational memory** for natural follow-up questions.
- **An MCP server** exposing the core tools.
- **A Flask dashboard** as the web front end.

> All employee and policy data in this project is fictional training data
> created for this exercise.

## Day 1 — What's implemented
- Git repository initialized on `feature/ai-travel-policy-assistant`.
- `src/ingestion.py`:
  - Loads all 7 policy `.txt` files, handling missing/empty files gracefully.
  - Cleans text (whitespace normalization, control-character stripping)
    while preserving numbered headings and bullet structure.
  - Tags every chunk with metadata: `source`, `policy_type`, `country`.
  - Chunks documents (800 chars / 150 overlap, paragraph-aware) — see
    `notebooks/chunking_notes.md` for why these values were chosen.
  - Outputs `data/chunks.json`, consumed by the embeddings step on Day 2.
- `src/prompts.py`: P.T.C.F., role-based, few-shot, and structured-output
  prompt templates for policy Q&A, trip validation, chunk classification,
  and structured extraction — reused and evaluated further on Day 5.

## Limitations (Day 1)
- No embeddings, retrieval, tools, agent, memory, MCP, or Flask app yet —
  these arrive on Days 2–4.
- The chunker is paragraph-aware but tuned for these short, well-structured
  policy files; longer or less structured documents would need re-tuning.
